## Concrete item listings

- Clothing listings now retain and display their concrete item definition, such
  as a mining helmet, instead of the generic `clothingitem` placeholder.
- Construction listings now retain and display their concrete definition and
  texture variant instead of the generic `constructionitem` placeholder.
- Concrete item icons are resolved in Marketplace views.
- Custom construction colors are preserved across listing, cancellation,
  rollback, purchase, delivery, and sale history.

The Marketplace database migrates automatically to schema version 4 by adding
backward-compatible nullable construction-color columns. Existing generic
listings remain readable, but their original concrete definition or color
cannot be reconstructed retroactively.
