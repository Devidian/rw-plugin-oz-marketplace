# OZ Marketplace 0.8.0

- Authorized managers can dress global and faction market criers using a random outfit from the world-local `crier-outfits` catalog or a copy of their currently worn clothing.
- Clothing changes do not take items from the player or alter the player's appearance. Existing appearance persistence restores selected clothes after reload.
- Existing criers retain their outfit until a manager chooses a new one.

Keep the Marketplace database and world-local outfit catalog when updating. Faction market criers require OZ - Factions 0.5.0 or newer. Requires Java 25 and Rising World PluginAPI 0.9.3.2.
