## Player markets, partial trades, and wanted listings

- Administrators can enable limited or unlimited player-owned markets.
- Market owners can manage only their own markets and receive their market tax
  revenue.
- Lost player-market Area links are repaired automatically at plugin startup.
- Buyers can purchase part of a listed stack or buy the complete remainder.
- Players can create wanted listings and fulfill them in multiple partial
  trades.
- Wanted items are delivered safely through OZ Mail after mailbox-capacity
  validation.
- Purchase dialogs show a live calculated unit price for the selected amount.
- Marketplace command, result, and dialog output is localized in German and
  English.

The Marketplace database migrates automatically to schema version 6. This
release requires OZ Tools 0.23.11 or newer. Wanted-listing fulfillment requires
OZ Mail 0.1.12 or newer and OZ Wallet.
