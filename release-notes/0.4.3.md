# OZ Marketplace 0.4.3

- Players can now view their own active offers and wanted listings in one place, including the original market location.
- Listings at a removed market zone or Market Crier are clearly marked and can still be withdrawn directly.
- The Marketplace footer shows how many active offers and wanted listings you have and your current limit.
- Global Marktschreier now expose the Global market tab whenever global trade is enabled for that Crier.
