## Marketplace capacity and private-market controls

- OZ Shop can now offer additional Marketplace listing capacity. Each purchase
  adds the configured base limit, so capacity grows from 1x to 2x, 3x, and so
  on.
- Administrators can configure the capacity base price and the price increase
  for later purchases.
- Unavailable private-market and global-mode actions are visibly disabled.
- Player-owned markets cannot change their global-trade mode.
- Capacity offer and purchase feedback are localized in German and English.

The capacity factor is stored as an additive player preference. Existing
Marketplace data remains compatible. This release requires OZ Tools 0.23.11 or
newer; the capacity offer requires OZ Shop and OZ Wallet.
